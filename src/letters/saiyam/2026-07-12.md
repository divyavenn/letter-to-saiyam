Dear Saiyam, 

I am writing this as you try to sleep with a splitting headache. 

Today we drove down to South Bay for guhrmehr’s birthday. god, it was sweltering! then skipped swing dancing for a couple of delightful hours in bed and bright gray evening at ocean beach. I wish I could bottle up some of that cold, blustering, achingly fresh sea-wind as a tonic. 

At the park I held ice cubes in my hands and pressed my wet palms into your burning skin. At home I heated up the eye compress. The warmth of it somehow made the mild headache you had much worse. 

You never accept assistance unless you’re pestered into it, so I can never tell if you don’t want to be a burden or if the “help” is actually a nuisance. Am I helping you or are you humoring me? It’s always the same tender eyes and the same mysterious little smile. I’m not complaining. 

I am learning to be in love is to be at someone’s mercy, to doubt yourself constantly, to drink deeply and greedily, licking the bowl long after the wine is done.  

I am afraid i want more of you than you can comfortably give. I get these mad urges to show you more and more of myself, even now when there’s hardly anything left to reveal. There’s something holy in all this selfishness and surrender. 

As the days go by I rack up many secrets, slightly shameful stories I’ll tell you in due time. I can already imagine how you’ll laugh; I already wonder what you’ll tell me in return.

Mostly, though, I just want your headache to go away and for it not to be bacterial meningitis. 

Yours and yours and yours, 
Divya
