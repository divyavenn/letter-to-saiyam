Dear Saiyam, 

I am writing this as you try to sleep with a splitting headache. 

Today we drove down to South Bay for guhrmehr’s birthday. god, it was sweltering! then skipped swing dancing for a couple of delightful hours in bed and a windy gray evening at ocean beach. I wish I could give you some of that cold, blustering, achingly fresh wind now, as a tonic. 

At the park I held ice cubes in my hands and pressed my wet cold palms into your arms and neck to cool you down. 

At home you had a headache, so I heated up the eye compress for you and massaged your head. The warmth of it somehow made the mild pain much worse. 

You never accept assistance 
 without me pestering, and I can never tell if it’s you not wanting to be a burden or if the “help” itself is a nuisance. Am I helping you or are you humoring me? It’s always the same tender eyes and the same mysterious little smile. I’m not complaining. 

As the days go by I rack up many secrets, slightly shameful stories I’ll tell you in due time. I can already imagine how you’ll laugh; I already wonder what you’ll tell me in return. 

I am learning to be in love is to be at someone’s mercy, to doubt yourself constantly, to drink deeply and greedily, licking the bowl long after the wine is done.  

I am afraid i want more of you than you have to give. I’d like you to have all the peace you need, though you have robbed me of mine. There’s this intermittent mad urge to show you more and more of myself, even now when there’s hardly anything left to reveal. There is something holy in all this selfishness and surrender. 

Mostly, though, I just want your headache to go away and for it not to be bacterial meningitis. 

Yours, 
Divya
