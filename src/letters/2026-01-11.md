Dear Saiyam, 

Happy 26th birthday! Our friendship has now officially spanned your twenties. You have a bit more free time, a bit less hair, and a slightly lower alcohol tolerance, but you are delightfully exactly the same.

Since your memory is (strategically) bad, here are some of my favorites of from past 3 years:

- The first real conversation we had. The group was taking a long late night walk from one party to another party, we were somewhat tipsy, and you were telling me about your life. I remember thinking “this is someone I want to get to know better” and almost regretting when we arrived. I think I told you I got married on that walk. actually rather unusual for me to tell someone that right off the bat. 

- telling kelvin wallace I was a dominatrix and that I met because your boss at Tesla was one of my clients. I was impressed at how rapidly you played along with the bit. You messed up only once when you accidentally called me by my real name 

- I would say when I almost got you beat up but frankly you were barely involved in that situation (sorry!) But you were so nice about it after, thank you for not hating me. 

- When you babysat my first shrooms trip! It felt like a bright new world after. There was a beautiful soft new snow falling. I have a crystal clear picture of how you laughed when I chased you down to hug you and of you riding off on your bike after. 

- The times you attended Book Club (reading aloud Aakash’s ex’s godawful memoir). I don’t know if you remember: the self-insert protagonist Laila was unceasingly self deprecating but all the other characters in the book seemed to think about/unwillingly admire/eroticize her nonstop. You said “She fucking knows we know SHE wrote this, right?” and it made me laugh very hard. 

- You cutting that little peg rail for me. It would have been simply impossible to execute my vision otherwise - the perfect proportions, the perfect color - and it meant a lot to me. It is currently packed up waiting to be shipped wherever I live next( no way I could leave it behind when I moved out of Gerrard!)

- Visiting you at the formula workshop

- Every time we went to honor snack bar but especially the first. Idk if I told you but they liked you so much they asked me about you the next time I came 

- All of our standup shows but especially the Punchline when we saw Gianmarco Soresi. The opener had a bit about how his mother talked way too much and would dominate any convo with excessively long stories filled w details he didn’t give two shits about. Fahd, utterly oblivious, whispered “you know, *my* mother’s kinda like that too!” We looked at each other and burst out laughing so hard we almost fell out of our chairs. It was the loudest laugh by *far* that the comedian had gotten and the look of confused gratification on his face only made me laugh harder. 

- A moss cloaked tree. clouds like the gates of heaven. A red red sunset. Frozen stars. A brief comet. A ripe golden moon.


Which brings me to my present: apparently even avid stargazers with extremely fancy telescopes will keep a pair of good binoculars for the convenience. They are as rugged and farsighted as you are. 

One of my favorite things about you is how you gravitate towards simplicity and subtlety, the harmonious and meditative. I’m so so grateful for what we’ve had, exactly as it’s been, whatever it may become. 

Love, 
Divya
