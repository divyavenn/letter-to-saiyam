Dear Saiyam,

I have very little to say. I need to get some bleach to clean the tea stain off my carpet. I have to finish painting the bathroom ceiling and put up the shower curtain hooks. I have to call a maid to clean the kitchen and wash Leo’s dishes. So much small mending and maintenance to do, always. I’m so tired of it. Perhaps I should sweep up my hair like Cinderella and dedicate one day entirely to it, all the admin work and errands and chores, and not think about it at ALL for the rest of the week. What bliss! Now that I have the logbook, I don’t have to hold it in my head anymore.

![Cinderella cleaning](/images/cinderella-cleaning.png)

I’d love sitting in this room and getting things done, even at a snail’s pace…if you were just a room away. I miss your hair and rubbing my nose against yours and the feeling of flannel under my cheek as I hug you. One day we’ll have your desk in the bedroom and mine in the living room. I can work while keeping an eye on the stove or hosting some friends. I put my japanese floor mattress under your desk and recline there with my head against your ankle when I read. Your feet will probably get too warm. Too bad.

Have I ever told you that you move and sit in an utterly efficient and unaffected way? You don’t affect nonchalance or stoicism, because unlike most you actually have those qualities.

My head hurts and I feel like a worm in its chrysalis, mostly mush. Still, I felt a bit better after I cleaned my room. Also, this whole time the perfect nightstand for your place has been at my house! The little side table I found on the sidewalk and lugged home one day, currently storing random odds and ends in our living room.

I write you these letters because it is easy when two people are rushed and busy to not have meaningful or raw conversations. I never want to feel like we’ve gotten disconnected, or that you don’t know me anymore.

Love you,
Divya
